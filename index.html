04 / Research Process

From Idea to
Global Publication

A proven six-phase methodology, refined across 247 published papers and hundreds of researchers. Every phase is mentored, reviewed, and optimized for acceptance.

01
Phase One · 2–4 Weeks

Topic Selection & Scoping

Work 1-on-1 with your assigned mentor to identify a high-impact, publishable research question. We audit existing literature gaps, assess dataset availability, and define a rigorous scope that maximizes your probability of acceptance at target journals.

  • Gap analysis in 50+ recent papers
  • Feasibility and novelty assessment
  • Target journal pre-selection
  • Research proposal drafting
02
Phase Two · 3–5 Weeks

Systematic Literature Review

Conduct a structured review of 50–200 relevant papers using Semantic Scholar, IEEE Xplore, and Google Scholar. Build a comprehensive related-work narrative that clearly positions your contribution relative to the state of the art.

  • Systematic search methodology
  • PRISMA-style screening
  • Related-work section drafting
  • Citation graph analysis
03
Phase Three · 2–4 Weeks

Dataset Collection & Preparation

Access curated real-world datasets through our institutional partnerships, or collect new data under mentor supervision. Learn preprocessing pipelines, data augmentation strategies, class balancing, and validation splitting to ensure experimental integrity.

  • Access to 30+ curated datasets
  • Preprocessing and augmentation
  • Statistical distribution analysis
  • Train/val/test split methodology
04
Phase Four · 4–8 Weeks

Model Design & Training

Design, implement, and iteratively train AI/ML models under expert technical supervision. Run ablation studies, baseline comparisons, hyperparameter searches, and full statistical significance testing to produce results that reviewers cannot reject.

  • Architecture design guidance
  • Ablation study design
  • Baseline comparison framework
  • Statistical significance testing
05
Phase Five · 4–6 Weeks

Academic Paper Writing

Craft a publication-ready paper in IEEE double-column or conference format. Our publication team provides three structured editing rounds — structural, technical, and linguistic — before any submission is made. Writing quality is non-negotiable.

  • IEEE / conference formatting
  • 3 rounds of dedicated editing
  • Figure and table polish
  • Abstract and keyword optimization
06
Phase Six · 4–12 Weeks

Submission & Publication

Submit to precisely targeted arXiv preprints, IEEE journals, or top-tier international conferences. We select the optimal venue, manage the submission portal, respond to reviewer comments with full revision support, and track your paper through to final acceptance and global indexing in Scopus and Web of Science.

  • Strategic journal selection
  • Reviewer response drafting
  • Revision and resubmission support
  • Scopus / WoS indexing confirmation

07 / Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to
Common Questions

No prior experience is required for the Starter Program or Research Program. We teach you everything from Python fundamentals to academic writing conventions. What we require is curiosity, commitment, and the ability to dedicate 8–15 hours per week. The NIW Track assumes you have some professional or academic background in your research domain.

Most participants complete the six phases in 6–12 months, depending on project complexity and individual pace. Journal peer review and acceptance adds an additional 2–4 months on average. The entire journey from enrolment to global publication typically spans 9–15 months.

We target arXiv preprints for early visibility, plus IEEE journals (TNNLS, TBME, RA-L, Transactions) and IEEE flagship conferences (EMBC, ICRA, ISCAS, ICCV). For education research: EDM, LAK, CHI. For logistics: Transportation Science, EJOR. Target venues are selected based on your paper's domain, scope, and quality level.

Hyundai Institute is an independent research organization, not a degree-granting university. Our output is real, globally-indexed publications bearing your name as co-author — which carries significantly more weight in academic admissions and immigration contexts than any internal certification. Our value is measured in publications, citations, and career outcomes, not credentials.

The National Interest Waiver (NIW) Track is designed for skilled foreign professionals who wish to obtain a US EB-2 green card based on their exceptional research contributions. We build a comprehensive portfolio: multiple publications, citation records, peer review evidence, and expert recommendation letters — all structured specifically for USCIS adjudication criteria. Our NIW approval rate is 94%.

Live mentor sessions are scheduled around your time zone and availability. We recommend 8–15 hours per week for steady progress; more is welcome, less is manageable for the Starter Program.

Research and NIW Track participants are covered by our publication commitment. If your paper is rejected, we analyze the reviewer feedback, revise the paper with you, and resubmit to an alternative venue at no additional charge. We have successfully placed papers that received initial rejections from top venues at equally prestigious alternative journals.
